# Research: Most Popular Resume Layout / CV Format in Indonesia

## Summary

Reverse-chronological ("chronological") is the dominant and most-recommended CV layout in Indonesia — every major Indonesian job platform (Jobstreet Indonesia, Indeed Indonesia, Glints, Kalibrr, KitaLulus, CakeResume) recommends it for most roles, with the combination/hybrid format a clear second for career-changers and senior roles; functional CVs are actively discouraged. Distinctively Indonesian expectations are a formal passport-style photo (3x4, neutral background), a personal-data block (place/date of birth, historically also religion and marital status — declining but still common), a 1–2 page length (1 page for fresh graduates), and conservative design (white background, dark text, one muted accent color). ATS-friendliness is now standard advice because the major Indonesian platforms process CVs digitally, but many SMEs still review manually, so the pragmatic local compromise is "simple ATS-safe layout **plus** photo."

## Findings

### 1. Layout ranking (focus: common resume layouts)

1. **Chronological (reverse-chronological) — dominant.** Indonesian platform guides and recruiter advice lead with this format: recruiters want to see career progression ("riwayat pekerjaan" in order, newest first) at a glance, and it is the easiest for both human scanning and ATS parsing. [Jobstreet Indonesia career advice](https://www.jobstreet.co.id/career-advice); [Indeed Indonesia career guide](https://id.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters); [Glints Blog](https://glints.com/id/blog/)
2. **Combination/hybrid — second, growing.** A skills/profile summary block on top + reverse-chronological experience. Recommended in Indonesian guides for career-changers, fresh graduates with little experience, and senior roles. [CakeResume Indonesia resources](https://www.cakeresume.com/resources); [Glints Blog](https://glints.com/id/blog/)
3. **Functional — actively discouraged.** Indonesian advice describes it as confusing for recruiters and poorly parsed by ATS; used only in niche cases. [Indeed Indonesia career guide](https://id.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters)

**Implication for a resume template:** default preset = reverse-chronological with an optional "ringkasan/profil" summary block (this is the local hybrid norm); offer functional only as an explicit edge-case option.

### 2. Standard section order in Indonesian CVs

The conventional local structure (in order): **Data Pribadi** (nama lengkap, contact, alamat, tempat/tanggal lahir; historically also agama + status pernikahan — now frequently omitted per modern advice), **Ringkasan/Profil** (2–3 lines), **Pendidikan**, **Pengalaman Kerja**, **Keahlian**; optional **Organisasi**, **Pelatihan/Sertifikasi**, **Referensi** ("tersedia atas permintaan"). A short profile summary at the top is a strong local norm. [Glints Blog](https://glints.com/id/blog/); [KitaLulus Blog](https://www.kitalulus.com/blog)

### 4. Indonesian-specific design preferences (focus: colors, photos, length)

- **Photo — the single biggest local differentiator.** A formal 3x4-style photo (professional attire, neutral background) is customary and still requested in many Indonesian job ads; platform guides phrase it "recommended in Indonesia, optional for ATS." Severity: **high** if omitted in a product targeting Indonesia — most Indonesian applicants will add a photo regardless. [Jobstreet Indonesia career advice](https://www.jobstreet.co.id/career-advice)
- **Length: 1 page (fresh grads / <5 years), 2 pages acceptable for experienced.** Indonesian recruiters tolerate two pages more than Western norms, but one page is the safe default; guides consistently cap at 2 pages. [Indeed Indonesia career guide](https://id.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters)
- **Colors/design: conservative.** White background, black/dark-navy text, one muted accent (blue/gray); Arial/Calibri/Times New Roman; avoid tables, multi-column layouts, graphics, and header/footer text when ATS matters. Creative industries (design, marketing, content) are the exception where more visual CVs are acceptable — so offer a "creative" preset but default to conservative. [Glints Blog](https://glints.com/id/blog/); [Kalibrr Advice](https://www.kalibrr.com/advice)
- **Language:** Bahasa Indonesia for local companies; English for multinationals/tech; bilingual CVs are common. The resume-editor's EN/ID export feature matches this local norm well. [Glints Blog](https://glints.com/id/blog/)

### 5. ATS compatibility in Indonesia (focus: ATS)

- ATS adoption is real and growing: Jobstreet Indonesia runs on SEEK's platform, Kalibrr parses uploaded CVs, Glints and KitaLulus scan applications, and mid-size+ companies use commercial ATS (Workable, Greenhouse, local vendors). Standard advice therefore matches global ATS rules: **standard section headings, single column, no text-in-images, PDF or DOCX, keywords from the job description, no photo-required disclaimer.** [Jobstreet Indonesia career advice](https://www.jobstreet.co.id/career-advice); [KitaLulus Blog](https://www.kitalulus.com/blog)
- **Local nuance:** many SMEs/startups still review CVs manually, so the Western "no photo" rule is not applied in Indonesia. The local consensus is **photo + simple ATS-safe layout**, not photo-free. A template should therefore keep the photo block but place it outside any tables/columns and keep the body single-column so parsers don't break.
- **Format caveat:** for ATS, export to PDF is fine; DOCX is the most parse-safe. Text must not be embedded in images.

### 6. Recruiter-preference evidence — data is scarce (focus: surveys)

- **Explicitly: no robust public survey ranks CV layouts by Indonesian recruiter preference.** This is a data gap, not a solved question.
- Best available evidence: (a) the platform-guidance consensus above (Jobstreet/SEEK, Indeed, Glints, Kalibrr, KitaLulus all converge on chronological + 1–2 pages + simple formatting); (b) Jobstreet/SEEK employer-facing content on hiring and CV screening; (c) the widely-cited (if dated) TheLadders eye-tracking claim that recruiters spend ~6 seconds on a first CV scan — repeatedly quoted in Indonesian career articles to justify scannable, chronological one-pagers. [TheLadders 2012 eye-tracking study as cited across Indonesian career blogs](https://www.theladders.com/press)

### 7. Implications for the resume-editor template (actionable)

1. **Default preset:** reverse-chronological, single column, conservative colors (white + black/navy + one blue/gray accent), Arial/Calibri.
2. **Mandatory-by-default section:** Data Pribadi with **optional photo block** (3x4 ratio hint, neutral background) — include photo but ensure it doesn't break ATS parsing (no text in image, photo outside text columns).
3. **Sections in local order:** ringkasan/profil → pendidikan → pengalaman kerja → keahlian → (organisasi, sertifikasi, referensi optional).
4. **Length guard:** one page default with warnings beyond 2 pages; font size ≥ 10pt.
5. **Language toggle:** EN/ID section headings (matches existing app feature).
6. **Export:** PDF default, DOCX option for maximum ATS compatibility.
